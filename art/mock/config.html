Security alerts

Once a day, by cron, the module looks up your Magento version and the Composer packages in composer.lock against public advisories (Packagist security advisories, CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities). Only package names and your Magento version are used to look things up; the matching happens here, on your server. No store URL, no customer data, no keys.

Comma-separated. Leave empty to see alerts in the admin only. Email is sent once per new finding, and again if it becomes exploited.

Exploited vulnerabilities are always emailed. Advisories Packagist has not rated count as High.
